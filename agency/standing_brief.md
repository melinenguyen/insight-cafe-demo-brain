# Standing brief: Sunday Cookie Co. (demo)

> A made-up cookie shop, so The Insight Café has something real-looking to research. The competitors are real; everything about Sunday Cookie is invented.

## What we want
- Win holiday gifting with boxes (6 and 12), not with discounts.
- One fresh idea a day that Sam can film or Mai can put in the shop.

## House rules
- Never discount the cookies. Free shipping on big boxes in December is the only offer.
- Talk like a friend at the counter: warm, a little cheeky, specific.
- Every idea needs a reason from the signals, not a hunch.
